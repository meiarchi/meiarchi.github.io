Organizer: Gradnja.rs · Year: 2025 · Type: Conceptual architectural competition

This regional competition challenged architects to design a modular prefabricated house composed of three interconnected modules of up to 50 m² each, responding to the changing needs of a growing family. My proposal asked: How can a home grow with life — not only in size, but in character, intimacy, and experience?

The concept is based on a modular timber system of glulam beams and columns. The first 50 m² module contains all essential functions for a couple and expands vertically through a mezzanine, creating additional spatial depth without increasing the footprint. As the family grows, new modules can be added horizontally or vertically, with the roof designed to be removed and reassembled as the structure evolves.

Each addition is conceived as a new stage of life — a child’s room, workspace, or place for retreat — while maintaining light, views, flexibility, and a consistent architectural language. Drawing on Japanese and Scandinavian influences, the project combines prefabrication, visible timber construction, and adaptable planning to create a living framework that can evolve together with its inhabitants.
