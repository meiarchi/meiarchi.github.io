title: 'Smederevo Waterfront'
---
Urban Regeneration · Public Space · Landscape Strategy

Location: Smederevo, Serbia · Year: 2021 · Type: Master’s Thesis

The project explores the regeneration of a neglected section of the Smederevo waterfront along the Danube, focusing on reconnecting the city, the historic fortress, and the river. The central question was: How can the waterfront become a continuous public landscape that strengthens both public access and the identity of Smederevo?

The proposal introduces a riverside promenade combined with green spaces, pedestrian connections, and public programs. Instead of one uniform park, the landscape is organized into different zones for recreation, gathering, culture, movement, and quieter interaction with the river.

Landscape, circulation, and public space were developed as one interconnected system, improving access between the city, fortress, and waterfront. Developed as my Master’s thesis under the mentorship of Prof. Aleksandra Đukić and Asst. Branislav Antonić, the project was also included in FLAGSHIP PROJECTS — Creative Urban Regeneration of Smederevo Waterfront.
