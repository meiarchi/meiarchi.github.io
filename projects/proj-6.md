Luxury Housing & Cantilevered Living Spaces

Location: Belgrade, Serbia · Year: 2018 · Type: University Project

Located in central Vračar, near the Temple of Saint Sava, the project explores contemporary urban housing through generous apartments, elevated outdoor spaces, and framed views toward the surrounding city and greenery. The central question was: How can outdoor space become an integral part of urban living rather than simply an addition to the apartment?

The concept is based on shifted volumes that extend beyond the main façade, forming large cantilevered terraces and outdoor rooms. An expressive steel structure supports these elements, while movable perforated metal panels provide solar protection and privacy, with patterns inspired by the architectural ornamentation of Saint Sava.

Open-plan interiors, extensive glazing, and direct access to the terraces blur the boundary between inside and outside, allowing the outdoor spaces to function as extensions of everyday living. The project was developed under the mentorship of Prof. Shin Yokoo, Prof. Vladimir Lojanica and Asst. Ksenija Pantović during the winter semester of 2018.
