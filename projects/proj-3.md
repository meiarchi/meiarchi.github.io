Earth-Sheltered Housing in Višnjička Banja
Location: Belgrade, Serbia · Year: 2017 · Type: University Project

Located on the sloping terrain of Višnjička Banja in Belgrade, the project explores an alternative model of residential development where housing, landscape, and food production form one integrated system. The central question was: What if the landscape surrounding a home could become productive rather than simply decorative?

The proposal follows the natural topography through elongated, earth-sheltered housing strips that reduce the visual impact of the buildings and allow the landscape to continue across green roofs. Each dwelling opens toward a private productive garden where residents can grow vegetables, herbs, and fruit, extending everyday living into the landscape.

The neighbourhood functions simultaneously as housing and cultivated terrain, combining productive gardens, biodiversity, water management, pedestrian spaces, and low-impact construction. Developed under the mentorship of Prof. Zoran Đukanović in 2017, the project explores themes of sustainable living, landscape integration, and architecture that responds directly to its context.
